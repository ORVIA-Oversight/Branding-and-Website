## Review rule

Reviewers must **not invent or resurrect a product** merely because a historical repository, domain, logo, page or SharePoint file exists.

They must distinguish:
- canonical public product;
- internal platform surface;
- specialist capability;
- legacy/archived concept;
- non-ORVIA adjacent business.

If they believe a supposedly canonical site should be merged or retired, they may challenge it and must state the operational/commercial consequence.
