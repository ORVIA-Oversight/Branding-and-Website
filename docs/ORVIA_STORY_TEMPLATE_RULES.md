# ORVIA STORY — TEMPLATE INHERITANCE RULES

## One company. Different doors.

ORVIA should not feel like a collection of unrelated products.

The parent story is ORVIA Oversight: when something does not feel right, somebody should look carefully before assumption hardens into decision.

Specialist sites are different doors into that same operating philosophy.

## The human progression

The founder story matters because the method was learned through operating environments, not invented as a software framework.

The progression is:

**frontline reality → operational responsibility → registered management → multi-site/regional leadership → strategic leadership → ORVIA**

That is why ORVIA repeatedly asks:
- what is actually happening?
- what does the evidence support?
- what is missing?
- who owns the next action?
- what is the human effect?
- did the fix actually work?

## The system underneath

Capture → IRIS → HIVE → VITA → VERA → accountable human judgement → action → effectiveness review.

This architecture should support the website story, not dominate it.

## The fixer proposition

Bring ORVIA the difficult, stuck or recurring problem before it becomes the crisis.

ORVIA does not make broken systems look better. It helps organisations see the operating reality, establish what the evidence supports, act on the pressure point and verify the improvement.

## Public language

Use calm, plain UK English.

Prefer:
- “The evidence supports…”
- “This remains unresolved…”
- “Here is what happens next…”
- “A human remains accountable…”

Avoid:
- revolutionary;
- military-grade;
- guaranteed;
- total protection;
- world-leading;
- AI will decide;
- unsupported scores or certainty.

## Final release test

A new page or site should answer:

1. What human/business problem is this for?
2. Why should the visitor care?
3. What exactly does ORVIA do?
4. What will ORVIA not do?
5. Why should the visitor trust it?
6. What is the next commercial or service action?
7. Who owns that action?
8. How does the work get verified?
9. Is the human still visible at the beginning and the end?
