# ORVIA WEBSITE MASTER BUILD STANDARD v1.3

**Status:** Candidate master for estate-wide migration  
**Owner:** ORVIA Oversight Ltd  
**Purpose:** Canonical build and replacement standard for public ORVIA websites and web applications.

## 1. Core rule

A public ORVIA site is an operating surface, not an isolated brochure.

Every site must help a first-time visitor answer, within 5–10 seconds:

1. What is this?
2. Is this for me?
3. What can it help with?
4. What do I do next?

Public pages explain outcomes, evidence, trust, culture and the customer journey. They do not expose proprietary engineering, prompts, orchestration, infrastructure topology, security design, deployment procedures or internal operating recipes.

## 2. Public narrative

Use:

**Problem -> consequence -> ORVIA response -> evidence/trust -> offer -> next action**

Prefer plain English over internal system names.

The user should not need to understand IRIS, HIVE, VITA, VERA or internal architecture to proceed.

## 3. ORVIA story

Across the estate, explain the common purpose consistently:

- information is often fragmented across people, systems and records;
- ORVIA helps bring the available evidence into a clearer shared picture;
- attribution, disagreement, missing evidence and professional boundaries remain visible;
- technology may organise, compare and support;
- authorised humans retain judgement, authority and accountability;
- material issues lead to owned actions and follow-through;
- improvement should be verified rather than assumed.

## 4. Controlled brand

Use Brand Guidelines v3.0 LIVE.

Core colours:

- Navy #0B2450
- Warm White #FAF7F2
- Gold #EAAA00
- Teal #2B929D
- Purple #82418F
- Orange #E74612

Use approved ORVIA marks only. Never redraw, recolour, distort or AI-generate the ORVIA logo.

Default visual world:

- warm white and navy;
- generous space;
- British documentary realism;
- human-first imagery;
- restrained functional accent colour;
- no default cyber, surveillance, military, hologram or futuristic-AI aesthetic.

## 5. Palette allocation

Allocate each product from the controlled palette library.

Do not invent a new palette for each build.

Current families:

- ORVIA Master
- Evidence Teal
- Decision Gold
- Insight Purple
- Action Orange
- Neutral Evidence
- Human Warm

The master logo, typography, canonical header/footer, accessibility rules and legal identity remain consistent.

## 6. Page structure

Major public product/service pages should normally contain:

1. Hero with clear proposition and primary CTA.
2. Who it is for / user problem.
3. What the service does.
4. Outcome or value.
5. How the public experience works.
6. Explainer image or visual where it reduces copy.
7. Trust / proof / boundaries.
8. Plain-English service information route.
9. Price / Buy / Start where fixed, or Discovery / Quote where genuinely scoped.
10. Customer onboarding or contact destination.
11. Related ORVIA routes and backlinks.
12. Canonical footer.

Depth must be available, not imposed.

## 7. Service information guides

Every meaningful public service should have a plain-English information page where useful.

It should explain:

- what the service is;
- who it is for;
- what it helps with;
- what working with it looks like;
- important boundaries;
- current live route;
- printable/saveable information.

The guide must not disclose ORVIA's proprietary implementation.

## 8. Media

Hero imagery:
- full-bleed or dominant where appropriate;
- documentary, realistic and human;
- deliberate negative space for copy;
- no fake text, signage, institutional branding or invented data.

Explainer imagery:
- scenario-led;
- communicates who is involved and what changes;
- reduces copy;
- can be reused as storyboard/keyframe material.

Short films:
- controlled branding;
- approved opener/closer where required;
- clear purpose;
- no invented logos, claims or metrics.

## 9. Public exposure

Do not publish:

- system prompts;
- agent orchestration logic;
- detailed infrastructure topology;
- internal endpoints;
- secrets or credentials;
- detailed security controls;
- recovery or deployment procedures;
- proprietary workflow mechanics beyond what the customer needs to understand;
- internal-only sales, release or monitoring architecture.

Public navigation should not route ordinary visitors into internal build, sales-operating or deployment references.

## 10. Employer brand

Public careers/employer content should communicate:

- meaningful work;
- high standards;
- evidence-led practice;
- learning and development;
- human judgement;
- professional boundaries;
- fair opportunity;
- veteran-friendly culture where supported;
- pride in the work.

Do not invent benefits, offices, customers, headcount, career guarantees or accreditation.

## 11. Commercial completion

A commercial site is not complete until the real journey works:

**Understand -> trust -> price/quote -> buy/start/contact -> onboarding -> workspace/workflow -> follow-up**

Fixed-price offer:
- visible approved price;
- real Buy / Start route;
- verified checkout/payment;
- verified success/onboarding destination.

Scoped service:
- Discovery / Request quote / Request proposal;
- lead captured;
- owner assigned;
- workflow/timer started;
- next action clear.

No decorative CTA.

## 12. Estate consistency

Every replacement site must inherit:

- ORVIA parent identity;
- allocated product palette;
- canonical header;
- approved canonical footer;
- task-appropriate navigation;
- trust layer;
- founder story source where relevant;
- ORVIA method;
- accessibility rules;
- legal layer;
- commercial completion gate;
- service-information pattern;
- public-exposure boundary;
- WEB-QA-01 release gate.

## 13. Release gate

WEB-QA-01 must verify 100% of applicable release gates.

Automatic HOLD for:

- wrong logo/header/footer;
- broken primary CTA;
- wrong price/payment route;
- unsupported claim;
- failed production deployment;
- broken critical navigation;
- materially broken mobile experience;
- unsafe AI authority claim;
- exposure of proprietary implementation detail.

100% = PASS  
95–99% = QUALIFIED HOLD  
Below 95% = HOLD

Repository code alone is not live proof.

## 14. Replacement-site migration method

For each site:

1. Preserve the valid proposition and evidence.
2. Remove stale or duplicated architecture.
3. Allocate controlled palette.
4. Apply canonical header/footer.
5. Rewrite the first screen in plain English.
6. Add outcome-led explainer content.
7. Add useful imagery/icons only where they clarify.
8. Add service information/download route.
9. Establish trust and boundaries.
10. Establish price/quote and real CTA.
11. Connect onboarding/workflow.
12. Verify cross-site backlinks.
13. Verify desktop/mobile/accessibility/SEO.
14. Verify production deployment.
15. Run WEB-QA-01 to 100%.

## 15. Design principle

**Do not make every website look identical. Make every website behave like ORVIA.**

MIA may feel warmer. Voice may feel responsive and commercial. Witness Room may feel calm and forensic. Threshold may feel serious and evidence-led. Web may feel faster and practical.

The common standard underneath must remain controlled.

## 16. Current master-state decision

The Brand & Web System is the candidate reference pattern.

Do not redesign it casually.

Future changes should be:
- evidence-backed;
- reconciled against Brand v3;
- checked for public exposure;
- tested commercially;
- passed through WEB-QA-01;
- versioned and recorded.
