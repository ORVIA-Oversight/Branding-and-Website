# ORVIA CORE FIXER MODEL — SITE INHERITANCE STANDARD

**Purpose:** preserve the core ORVIA operating story across every product, service and website without forcing every homepage to explain the entire operating architecture.

## Source basis

This standard distils the current public ORVIA Oversight site into the reusable principles that should survive future rebuilds.

The live public site establishes that ORVIA exists for situations where something does not feel right and somebody needs to look carefully; it describes ORVIA as independent, human-centred oversight that organises, reviews and prepares evidence so the appropriate professional can understand the matter and act within their role.

It also establishes several recurring ideas:

- look beneath policy, paperwork, dashboards and internal assurance;
- understand the whole picture rather than a single record;
- use evidence preparation before professional action;
- maintain named human accountability at every gate;
- do not automatically accept every enquiry;
- redirect honestly when a solicitor, regulator, statutory service, clinician or other professional is the right route;
- define scope, limitations, fee and outputs before substantive work begins;
- keep the person at the centre;
- separate the public story, workflow status and evidence stores;
- do not hide behind AI or process.

## The ORVIA Fixer Model

**Bring ORVIA the difficult, stuck or recurring problem before it becomes the crisis.**

ORVIA does not exist to make broken systems look better. It exists to help people and organisations:

1. see what is really happening;
2. identify the pressure point;
3. test the available evidence;
4. separate evidence from assumption;
5. make gaps and uncertainty visible;
6. decide what proportionate action belongs with whom;
7. implement the fix;
8. verify whether the improvement actually worked.

This is the backbone that should survive across all products.

## Human First. Human Last.

Every system begins with a person and ends with a person.

Technology may capture, organise, compare, route, preserve provenance and challenge assumptions. It must not become the final authority over another human being in safeguarding, clinical, culpability or other high-consequence decisions.

The governance question beneath every ORVIA product is:

> **What is the likely effect of this decision on the person at the centre?**

## Reusable ORVIA operating story

**SIGNAL → SUITABILITY → SCOPE → REVIEW → ACTION → ASSURANCE**

- **Signal:** what does not feel right?
- **Suitability:** is ORVIA the right route and is the work safe, in-scope and conflict-free?
- **Scope:** what exactly will ORVIA do, what will it not do, what will it cost and who owns the next step?
- **Review:** what does the available evidence actually support, what does it not support and what remains unresolved?
- **Action:** what should happen next, and who is accountable for it?
- **Assurance:** was the action implemented, effective and sustained?

This sits underneath the current ORVIA Method:

**OBSERVE → REVIEW → VERIFY → INTERPRET → ACT**

The public website being distilled here uses older method labels in places. Those labels are not carried forward into this standard; the current 1 October 2026 O/R/V/I/A method remains authoritative.

## How every ORVIA product site inherits this

Every site should be recognisably ORVIA without becoming the same website.

The homepage sells the specific product. The deeper ORVIA system earns trust underneath it.

### Above the surface — product-specific

- the problem;
- the audience;
- the promise;
- the story;
- product imagery;
- examples;
- pricing or quote route;
- onboarding route;
- product-specific proof.

### Underneath — inherited from ORVIA

- ORVIA Oversight parent identity;
- evidence before assumption;
- human first / human last;
- named human accountability;
- scope and boundary discipline;
- calm, non-pressure commercial approach;
- provenance and traceability;
- proportionate redirection when ORVIA is not the right route;
- action followed by verification.

## Required commercial journey

**UNDERSTAND → TRUST → PRICE/QUOTE → BUY/START → ONBOARD → DELIVERY → FOLLOW-UP → RETENTION/EXPANSION**

A site is not complete because it looks finished. It is complete when the buyer can move through the entire route without dead ends.

## What must not drift

Future products and websites must not:

- turn ORVIA into generic consultancy language;
- present technology or AI as the hero;
- automate consequential judgement;
- imply ORVIA replaces professionals or statutory bodies;
- force a new buyer to learn IRIS/HIVE/VITA/VERA before understanding the product;
- use pressure-selling, false urgency or scarcity;
- treat an action plan as proof that change happened;
- lose the human being inside the process.

## Template rule

**One frozen template + one structured product brief + approved assets = one finished ORVIA site.**

The structured brief supplies the product-specific story. This core model supplies the inherited ORVIA operating philosophy.

That is how the architecture survives future rebuilds without every team or AI model reinventing ORVIA.
