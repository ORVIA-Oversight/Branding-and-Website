THE ORVIA METHOD

O.R.V.I.A.

The ORVIA method moves through Observe, Review, Verify, Interpret and Act. It connects records with lived reality instead of treating paperwork as the whole picture.

Explore the ORVIA methodBook an ORVIA review
OBSERVE

What is happening? Gather the records, accounts, context and direct evidence.

REVIEW

Review the evidence in context, including lived experience, practice and what may be missing.

VERIFY

Check, compare and identify what is supported, missing, disputed or contradictory.

INTERPRET

Interpret patterns and significance without claiming more than the evidence supports.

ACT

Turn verified understanding into practical, proportionate and accountable action.

THE GRACE PRINCIPLE · Assume positive intent where reasonable · Seek to understand before blame · Be fair, respectful and proportionate · Keep people at the centre · Create constructive change
