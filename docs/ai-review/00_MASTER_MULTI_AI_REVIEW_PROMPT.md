## Non-negotiable architecture

ORVIA means:

**O — OBSERVE**
**R — REVIEW**
**V — VERIFY**
**I — INTERPRET**
**A — ACT**

Every ORVIA public website must feel like one organisation: **ORVIA Oversight** as the fixed parent identity, then the active system icon/name beside it.

The homepage of each ORVIA business/product must sell that product clearly, but the deeper ORVIA fundamentals stay behind the experience:

- evidence before assumption;
- human first, human last;
- authorised humans retain consequential judgement;
- shared ORVIA trust/legal layer;
- shared navigation and estate switcher;
- shared O/R/V/I/A method logic;
- shared CTA / commercial rules;
- shared accessibility and mobile rules;
- shared IRIS/customer handoff logic;
- shared provenance / HIVE / verification philosophy;
- shared founder story and brand boundaries where relevant.

Do **not** turn every homepage into an explanation of the internal architecture.

## Commercial philosophy

ORVIA must sell **without pressure**.

The commercial path is:

**UNDERSTAND → TRUST → PRICE/QUOTE → BUY/START → ONBOARD → DELIVERY → FOLLOW-UP → RETENTION/EXPANSION**

Commercial design must:
- make the next step obvious;
- show fixed prices where the offer is genuinely fixed;
- use discovery/proposal routes where scope genuinely varies;
- avoid fake urgency, countdowns, manipulative scarcity or aggressive sales copy;
- make it easy to research before buying;
- let the user move quickly to purchase when ready;
- keep claims bounded and evidenced.

## Story-driven rule

A website is created from its story, not from a page list.

Before design, understand:

1. Why does this business/product exist?
2. What problem is it trying to solve?
3. What happens if that problem is ignored?
4. What has the organisation learned?
5. What did it build/change in response?
6. What does the customer get?
7. What can be proved?
8. What must be included?
9. What must never be claimed or shown?
10. What action should the visitor take next?

The website then becomes an elegant storyboard:

**Hero → Why → Problem → Insight → Response → Offer → Proof → Action**

## Current visual direction

Use the latest ORVIA brand standard:
- warm white + ORVIA navy as the dominant visual system;
- documentary-quality British imagery;
- restrained O/R/V/I/A accent colours;
- simple rounded human/evidence/assurance icon language;
- concise cards;
- generous breathing space;
- no cyberpunk, military, surveillance, generic AI brains or “mission control” visual language;
- technology supports the story, it is not the hero.

The homepage should feel premium and editorial, not like a dashboard.

## Review the live site

Review https://www.orvia.org.uk/ in full.

Treat it as useful evidence of what already works, not as untouchable truth.

Identify:
- what should survive;
- what is duplicated;
- what is too text-heavy;
- what needs an icon;
- what needs a photograph;
- what needs a diagram;
- what should become video;
- what should be removed;
- what belongs deeper in the site;
- whether Voice and Web are commercially visible enough;
- whether the founder story is strong enough;
- whether the site explains ORVIA in five seconds;
- whether the current content sells calmly rather than pressuring;
- whether each CTA leads to a real next step.
